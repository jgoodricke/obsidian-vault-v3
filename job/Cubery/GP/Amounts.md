## Scripts to use

### If they make a very low offer
> Thank you. I’ve heard the offer, but it’s too low to resolve the matter today. Given the timing, the escalation that followed, and the cost and risk of continuing, that figure does not reflect the real exposure here. If the employer is willing to move meaningfully, I’m prepared to keep discussing resolution.

### If they improve, but are still too low
> I appreciate the movement. We are still some way apart, but I’m willing to continue negotiating in good faith if the employer is prepared to move further.

### If I want to signal the real zone
> I’m not expecting the employer to accept my opening position, but I do expect an offer that reflects the real litigation risk and the value of finality. If the employer can move into a more realistic range, there is a genuine prospect of resolution.

### If they keep saying “performance only”
> I understand that is the employer’s position. My position is that the timing and sequence of events create a real issue about reasons, and that is why there is litigation risk here. I’m not asking the employer to concede liability today. I’m asking the employer to resolve that risk commercially.

### If they repeat the same low number
> I understand that is the employer’s current position. Unless there is meaningful movement, it may be that we are not going to resolve the matter today. I’m still willing to negotiate, but only if the discussion is moving towards a realistic outcome.

### If they move into acceptable territory
> Thank you, that is a more realistic offer. Subject to the settlement terms being recorded properly, including the non-monetary terms we discussed, I’m prepared to work towards finalising this today.

### If I decide to walk away
> I’ve participated in good faith and remain open to resolving the matter, but the employer’s position is still too far from a realistic settlement range. Unless there is a substantially improved offer, I don’t think settlement is possible today.

## Negotiation discipline
- Stay calm
- Do not argue every factual detail
- Do not drop too quickly
- Make smaller moves than they expect
- Trade concessions, do not give them away
- Keep coming back to risk, cost, and finality
- Do not insult anyone personally, even if tempted

## Non-monetary

**Essential**
- release limited to this claim only, or explicit carve-out preserving unpaid wages / overtime / super / statutory entitlement claims
- payment due within a short fixed period
- confidentiality with normal carve-outs for legal, tax, medical and immediate family discussions

**Nice to have**
- mutual non-disparagement
- neutral statement that employment ended by resignation, if wording becomes contentious
- confirmation all property and payroll admin are finalised

**Probably not worth spending much capital on**
- statement of service, unless you suddenly decide you want it
- apology
- detailed admissions
- rewriting the whole performance history.

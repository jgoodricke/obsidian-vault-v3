As a starting point, below is a list of what we would be looking at for Schedule Mee and Carebridge Integrations with CRM's. We'd be looking at starting off with Hubspot, Resi Select and Sugar. 

## CRM to Schedule Mee

**Resident Details**

- First Name

- Last Name

- Room Number

- Facility

- Wing

- Date of Birth
- Gender
- Admission Date

- Expected Discharge (if applicable)

- About Me profile

### Service Information

- Service Name

- Service Frequency

- Commencement Date

- End Date (if applicable)

## Schedule Mee to CRM

### Service History

- Resident Name

- Service Name

- Service Date

- Service Time

- Service Status

- Service Notes

### Analytics/Reports

- Services delivered this month

- Missed/Refused/Incomplete/Cancelled visits

- Companion hours

- Service completion rate

- Refusal trends

- Outstanding services

### Carebridge to CRM
- New referrals
- Referral updates
- Documents
- Patient demographics
- Clinical summary
- Messaging (comments added to enquiry by hospital)

### CRM to Carebridge (Nice-to-Have, we can do this the other way isntead if easier)
- Enquiry status changes
- Admission outcome
- Date admitted
- Information requests and messaging (comments added to enquiry by provider)

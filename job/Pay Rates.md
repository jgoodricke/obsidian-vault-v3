# Base Salary Calculation
## TL/DR
Full Package = salary + Leave (11.95%) + super (11.5%).


 To calculate the equivalent hourly rate for your side job that accounts for lost benefits from your main job, we need to factor in both paid leave entitlements and superannuation. Here's the breakdown:

### 1. **Paid Leave Adjustment**

**Total paid days:**  
251 working days + 30 leave days = **281 paid days**

**Leave multiplier:**  
281/251≈1.1195251281​≈1.1195

**Adjusted rate (with leave):**  
$49.93×1.1195≈$55.88

---

### 2. **Superannuation Adjustment**

**Superannuation multiplier:**  
1+11.5/100=1.115

**Final equivalent rate:**  
$55.88×1.115≈$62.31$55.88×1.115≈$62.31

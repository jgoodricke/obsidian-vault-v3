I have been married for 4 years. My wife and I have had basically no sex during the marriage. I have tried talking with her about it many times, but she does not seem to care. After months, she agreed to have sex, but it felt awful because she said beforehand she would not enjoy it, and I felt like she deliberately made sure she did not enjoy it to prove a point.

***Counter-point / caution:***  
*That is how it felt to me, and the experience was painful and humiliating. But I should be careful about assuming I know her exact motive. The safer and more grounded version is: she told me she would not enjoy it, the sex felt unwanted and awful, and I do not want sex like that again. Whether or not she was trying to prove a point, the important thing is that sex under those conditions is not healthy or desirable for either of us.*

She often seems to withdraw sex as punishment for perceived slights. If I do something that annoys her, it can mean “no sex” for another two weeks, which effectively means we almost never have sex. Recently I asked if we could have sex, and she became aggressive and said she does not think we will ever have sex again.

***Counter-point / caution:***  
*It may feel like sex is being withdrawn as punishment, and that pattern may be real. But saying “you use sex as punishment” directly to her may escalate the conflict. A less inflammatory way to describe it is: “It feels like affection and sexual intimacy disappear whenever there is conflict, and there is no clear path back to closeness.” That keeps the focus on the pattern and its effect on me, rather than trying to prove her intent.*

I am very tired of the constant rejection, drama, and lack of intimacy. I do not want to pressure her into sex, and I understand sex must be freely wanted by both people. But I also do not want to pretend I am okay with a marriage that has no intimacy, affection, or repair.

Divorce does not feel like an option right now because we have a young son, and I am afraid of being separated from him or dragged into a messy co-parenting situation. I grew up around messy divorces and know how badly things can go when one parent wants to make the other parent’s life difficult. For now, I would rather maintain a strong daily relationship with my son, even if my relationship with my wife is terrible.

***Counter-point / caution:***  
*Wanting to stay close to my son every day is understandable and important. But I should not assume that staying in the same household automatically protects him. A tense, cold, hostile, or emotionally unsafe home can also hurt a child over time. The goal should not simply be “stay no matter what”. The goal should be to create the most stable, respectful, emotionally safe environment possible, whether that means staying together, sleeping separately, living as co-parents under one roof for a time, or eventually separating.*

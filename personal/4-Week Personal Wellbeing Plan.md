**Goal:** Reduce emotional pain, manage sexual frustration, maintain self-respect, and improve my quality of life while remaining committed to my marriage.

## Week 1 — Reduce Rejection and Emotional Pain

- [ ] **Stop initiating sex and unwanted kisses**
  - Respect my wife's boundaries.
  - Avoid repeatedly putting myself in situations where I experience rejection.

- [ ] **Stop looking for signs that sex might happen**
  - Don't analyse every hug, kind gesture, or good mood as a potential invitation.
  - Avoid building expectations around physical intimacy.

- [ ] **Continue ordinary affection when welcome**
  - Remain warm, kind, and loving.
  - Only initiate hand-holding or cuddling when mutually comfortable.
  - Don't expect affection to lead to sex.

- [ ] **Find a private outlet for sexual frustration**
  - Consider masturbation if consistent with my values.
  - Accept that having sexual needs is normal.
  - Avoid feeling guilty or ashamed of my sexuality.

- [ ] **Start journaling about my emotions**
  - Spend 10 minutes writing about how I'm feeling.
  - Identify whether I'm experiencing loneliness, rejection, sexual frustration, anger, or hopelessness.
  - Avoid dwelling excessively on negative thoughts.

## Principles to Remember

> [!important] My needs matter
> Wanting sexual and romantic intimacy with my wife is legitimate. I do not need to feel ashamed of those needs, even when they cannot currently be met.

> [!important] Respect boundaries without abandoning myself
> My wife has the right to decline intimacy. I also have the right to acknowledge that its absence hurts me.

> [!important] Don't try to earn affection
> Exercise, self-improvement, household responsibilities, and kindness should not be strategies for obtaining sex. I should pursue them because they reflect the person I want to be.

> [!important] Don't punish my wife
> I can stop initiating unwanted intimacy without becoming cold, resentful, or deliberately withdrawing affection.

> [!important] I cannot fix this alone
> I can improve my wellbeing and contribute positively to my marriage, but rebuilding a mutually satisfying sexual relationship requires willingness from both partners.

> [!important] Live my life now
> I do not need to postpone my happiness until my wife wants intimacy again. I can continue building a meaningful and fulfilling life while acknowledging that something important is missing.

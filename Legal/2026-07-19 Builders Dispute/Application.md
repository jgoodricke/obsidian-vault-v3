## Description of the dispute 
We are the current owners of 9 Furness Close, Wyndham Vale. The dwelling was constructed by Symmetric Homes.

A visual Property and Timber Pest Inspection dated 9 June 2026 recorded significant cracking to the garage concrete slab, Category 2 cracking to external brickwork, inadequate surface grading and drainage with water ponding near the dwelling, elevated moisture or possible waterproofing concerns in the bathroom, possible window water ingress, and the additional items listed in the attached defect schedules.

The inspection report did not conclusively determine causation and recommended further specialist assessment of the cracking.

We notified Symmetric Homes of the reported defects in writing on 11 June. Symmetric Homes was provided with reasonable access and arranged an inspection of the property on 2 July 2026.

Following repeated written requests for its findings, Symmetric Homes acknowledged cracking, minor settlement, inadequate falls and water ponding, but denied responsibility. It alleges that landscaping, paving or other work undertaken after practical completion altered the original drainage grades and caused the observed movement.

Symmetric Homes has not provided the requested inspection summary or photographs, measurements of the cracking, level surveys, practical-completion photographs or level records, original drainage records, or other material substantiating its causation conclusion. Although its correspondence stated that an inspection summary and photographs were attached, no such attachments were provided.

Symmetric Homes has asserted that the dwelling is more than six years old and is therefore no longer covered by warranty. We dispute that assertion. The occupancy permit was issued on 20 April 2021, meaning that, as at 1 August 2026, the building work is approximately five years and three months old. In addition, we understand that the six-year period may relate to domestic building insurance coverage for structural defects and is not necessarily the expiry of the builder’s statutory implied warranties or responsibility for defective domestic building work. We request that the BPC consider the applicable warranty and limitation provisions.

The parties therefore dispute:

1. whether the garage slab cracking, external wall cracking, movement, drainage and moisture conditions arise from defective or incomplete original building work;
2. whether original site preparation, surface drainage, slab or footing work contributed to the conditions;
3. whether post-completion landscaping or other later work altered the original ground levels and, if so, the extent to which that contributed; and
4. what investigation and rectification are reasonably required.

Symmetric Homes has denied responsibility and has not offered any further substantiated investigation or rectification proposal.

We remain willing to provide reasonable access for inspection, photography, measurements and non-invasive assessment. Before permanent crack repairs, repointing, filling, slab injection or other work that may alter or conceal the existing conditions, we seek a properly documented assessment of the likely underlying cause and a written rectification scope. We remain willing to consider agreed temporary protective or drainage measures required to prevent further damage
## Suggested outcome requested
We seek:
1. a BPC building assessment of the garage slab cracking, external wall cracking, movement, site drainage, ground levels, water ponding, bathroom moisture concerns, possible window water ingress and the additional items listed in the attached schedules;
2. measurement and classification of the cracking and an assessment of whether the relevant domestic building work is defective or incomplete;
3. an assessment of the likely underlying cause, including whether original construction, site preparation, surface drainage, slab or footing work, subsequent landscaping, or a combination of causes contributed;
4. identification of any further investigation reasonably required, including level surveys, plumbing or drainage investigation, structural assessment or monitoring;
5. a written rectification scope that addresses any established underlying cause before cosmetic crack repair or repointing;
6. facilitation of a written agreement, or if necessary an appropriate dispute resolution order, requiring Symmetric Homes to complete any work for which it is found responsible within specified timeframes; and
7. confirmation that the current cracking and relevant drainage conditions should be documented before permanent work that would materially alter or conceal the available evidence.

We are the current owners of 9 Furness Close, Wyndham Vale. The dwelling was constructed by Symmetric Homes.

A property inspection dated 9 June 2026 identified significant cracking to the garage concrete slab, Category 2 cracking to external brickwork, inadequate grading and drainage around the dwelling with water pooling near the slab and foundations, and moisture or waterproofing concerns.

We notified Symmetric Homes in writing and provided reasonable access for an inspection on 2 July 2026. Following repeated written requests, Symmetric Homes acknowledged cracking, minor settlement, inadequate falls and water ponding, but denied responsibility. It alleges that landscaping undertaken after practical completion altered the original drainage grades and caused the movement.

Symmetric Homes has not provided measurements, level surveys, original drainage or ground-level records, or a properly substantiated causation assessment. Its email stated that an inspection summary and photographs were attached, but no attachments were provided.

The parties therefore dispute whether the cracking, movement, drainage and moisture conditions result from defective or incomplete original building work, original drainage or site preparation, subsequent landscaping, or a combination of causes. Symmetric Homes has declined further investigation and rectification.

# Key timing and record discrepancies

**Initial call.** James's phone record shows a call from Maples at 10:34 am, while Maples recorded the incident at 10:36 am and parent notification at 10:42 am. If the phone record is accurate, Maples' recorded sequence cannot be correct.

**Collection.** The family records collection at about 10:53 am. Maples records 10:58 am. This may reflect different times for James's arrival, the handover or signing the incident acknowledgement.

**Medical attention.** The incident report recorded that medical attention was not sought. James took Jackson directly to a GP and then to the Royal Children's Hospital on the day of the injury. Surgery occurred the following day.

**Plastic surgeon timing.** The surgeon's letter is dated 15 July 2026 and describes the injury as approximately three weeks earlier ('3/52 ago'). The documented incident date was 15 June 2026, one month earlier. The wording appears approximate.
